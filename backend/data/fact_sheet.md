# Lumora Cloud Fact Sheet (Effective 1 September 2026)

## 1. Company
- Lumora Cloud was founded in 2018 and is headquartered in Pune, India.
- More than 12,000 business customers use Lumora Cloud as of September 2026.
- Lumora Cloud operates data centers in 6 regions: Mumbai, Singapore, Frankfurt, Dublin, Virginia, and Sydney.

## 2. LumoraSync Pricing
- Starter plan: $12 per user per month, billed annually, with 1 TB of storage per user.
- Business plan: $24 per user per month, billed annually, with 5 TB of storage per user.
- Enterprise plan: custom pricing, with unlimited storage under a fair-use policy.
- Free trial: 14 days, no credit card required.
- Prices exclude applicable taxes.

## 3. Reliability and Performance
- The uptime SLA is 99.9% per calendar month for Business and Enterprise plans.
- Starter plans have no financially backed SLA.
- Average file sync latency is 180 ms in internal benchmarks run in August 2026.
- LumoraSync syncs large files up to 2.4x faster than the previous version, LumoraSync 3, in internal benchmarks run in August 2026.

## 4. Security and Compliance
- Lumora Cloud holds SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications.
- HIPAA compliance support is available on Enterprise plans only.
- Data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
- Lumora Cloud does not hold FedRAMP authorization and has no FedRAMP assessment in progress.

## 5. Support
- 24/7 chat support is included on Business and Enterprise plans.
- Starter plans receive email support with a 1 business day response target.

## 6. Retired Figures (Do Not Use)
- Retired: the $8 per user per month Starter price applied until 31 December 2025 and is no longer valid.
- Retired: the 99.99% uptime figure was never an official SLA and must not be used.
- Retired: the 8,000 customers figure was current in 2024 and is outdated.
