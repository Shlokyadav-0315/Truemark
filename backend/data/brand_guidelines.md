# Lumora Cloud Brand and Communications Guidelines (v3.2)

## 1. Brand Voice

### 1.1 Principles
Lumora Cloud speaks like a knowledgeable colleague: clear, confident, and plain-spoken. We explain before we persuade. We never shout.

### 1.2 Informal Language
Do not use slang, text-speak, or contractions such as "gonna" and "wanna" in customer-facing copy. Limit exclamation marks to one per piece. Never use multiple exclamation marks in a row.

### 1.3 Emoji
Emoji are permitted on social channels only, and no more than two per post. They are not permitted in press releases, emails to customers, or product documentation.

## 2. Prohibited Language

### 2.1 Banned Buzzwords
The following words and phrases must not appear in any external copy: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "game changer", "best-in-class", "cutting-edge", "world-class", "next-generation", "unbeatable", "disruptive", "synergy". Describe what the product does instead.

### 2.2 Competitor References
Never name a competitor in marketing copy. Competitor names (including Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Box, and iCloud) may be referenced only by Legal in approved comparison documents. Refer to "other providers" instead.

### 2.3 Absolute Guarantees
Never promise "100%" uptime, "zero downtime", "unbreakable" security, or "guaranteed" results. Our SLA is stated in the Fact Sheet and must be quoted exactly.

## 3. Claims and Substantiation

### 3.1 Superlatives
Superlatives such as "fastest", "best", "most secure", "number one", "#1", and "leading" require written substantiation approved by Legal. Without it, use factual descriptions instead.

### 3.2 Numbers and Statistics
Every figure, including prices, storage amounts, customer counts, and uptime percentages, must match the current Fact Sheet exactly. Outdated figures must be removed before publication.

### 3.3 Security and Compliance Claims
Only certifications listed in the Fact Sheet may be mentioned. Do not imply certifications that are pending, in progress, or not held.

## 4. Required Disclaimers

### 4.1 Pricing
Any copy that states a price must include the line "Prices exclude applicable taxes."

### 4.2 Performance
Any copy that states a speed or performance figure must include "Based on internal benchmarks" and the month and year of the test.

## 5. Naming and Style

### 5.1 Product Names
Our product is written "LumoraSync" as one word with a capital L and S. Never "Lumora Sync", "Lumorasync", or "lumorasync". The company name is "Lumora Cloud".

### 5.2 Accessible Link Text
Never use "click here" or "read more" as link text. Link text must describe the destination.
